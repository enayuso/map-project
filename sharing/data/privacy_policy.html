Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

BelizeMap ("the App," "we," "us") is a map, navigation, and local-information app for Belize. This policy explains what personal data the App collects, why, and what rights you have over it — under the laws applicable to you, including Belize's Data Protection Act, 2021.

1. What we collect

Most of the App — map browsing, search, directions, ferry/dive-site/border-crossing information, weather, and viewing hazard reports and place corrections submitted by others — works with no account and no personal data collected at all.

If you choose to create an account, or use a feature that needs one, we collect:

  • Email address and password (if you sign up with email/password) — your password is never stored in plain text; only a one-way cryptographic hash of it is kept, which cannot be reversed back into your actual password, even by us.
  • An Apple-provided identifier (if you sign in with Apple instead) — we never request or receive your real name or email through Sign in with Apple.
  • A nickname and avatar you choose — never your real name. There is no way to enter a real name anywhere in the App.
  • Precise device location — only while you actively use a location-based feature: sharing your live location with people you choose, reporting or viewing road hazards near you, or getting directions. Location is not collected in the background for any other purpose, and never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of your movements over time.
  • Content you submit — road hazard reports and suggested corrections to place listings, if you choose to submit any. These are reviewed by a person before being shown as verified information to other users.

2. What we don't do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with data brokers or any other third party for their own purposes.
  • We do not link anything you do in this App with data from any other app, website, or company.

3. Why we process this data

Email/password and account data are processed because they're necessary to create and operate your account. Live location sharing is a separate, optional feature you turn on yourself — it is used only to show your location to people you've actively chosen to share it with, for as long as you keep sharing active, and it stops the moment you turn it off. Hazard reports and place corrections are processed to show useful, community-sourced information to other users of the App, subject to review.

4. How long we keep it

If you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately: you're signed out on every device right away, and your email, password, and any other sign-in information are removed from the record straight away, making that email address available for a new account immediately. The remaining anonymized record is permanently and irreversibly erased after 30 days. This short window exists purely as a safety net against an accidental or coerced deletion — it is not a way to recover a deleted account yourself; if you need to undo a deletion within that window, contact us directly.

Hazard reports and place corrections may remain visible after you delete your account, since they're shared, community information rather than personal data about you specifically — they are not linked back to your identity once your account is gone.

5. Where your data is processed

The App's backend is operated outside Belize. Wherever your data is processed, we apply the same protections described in this policy.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, restrict, object to, or request a copy of your personal data, in addition to erasing it. Most of these can be exercised directly in the App: view your account details, edit your nickname or avatar, or delete your account entirely, at any time, from the Profile screen. 7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you are between 13 and the age of majority where you live, you may need a parent or guardian's permission to use the App, depending on local law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including one-way password hashing and encrypted network connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and, where required, the relevant authority without undue delay if we become aware of a breach affecting your data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects how your data is handled, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the App.
